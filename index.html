About Me

Dia duit! I'm a passionate Irish language educator with over a decade of experience teaching learners of all levels. My mission is to make Irish accessible, engaging, and enjoyable for everyone who wishes to connect with this beautiful language and culture.

I believe that language learning is a journey, not a destination. Each student brings their own goals, challenges, and unique learning style, and I'm here to tailor my teaching approach to help you succeed. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to refine your fluency, I provide personalized lessons that respect your pace and celebrate your progress.

Beyond the classroom, I'm deeply committed to Irish culture and heritage. I draw upon traditional stories, music, and customs to enrich the learning experience. My approach combines modern teaching methods with a deep respect for the language's historical roots, creating a balanced and meaningful educational experience.

When I'm not teaching, you'll find me exploring Irish literature, practicing traditional music, or simply enjoying the beauty of the Irish language in everyday conversation. I'm excited to help you discover why Irish has captured my heart, and why it can become a cherished part of your life too.
